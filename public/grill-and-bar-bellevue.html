Straight answers

Before you order

What kind of food does the barn serve?

A country kitchen doing burgers and signature grill plates — the restaurant half of a country bar, restaurant, and live music destination.

What's drinking?

A full beer list and a cocktail round, poured from open until the early hours on Fridays and Saturdays.

Is it expensive?

No — the venue's published price band is $$, the moderately-priced range. Menu pricing is in-house.

When can I eat there?

With the bar, every open day: Wed 11 AM–11 PM, Thu 11 AM to midnight, Fri–Sat 11 AM–2 AM. Closed Sunday through Tuesday.

Is it a full restaurant or just a bar?

Both — a country bar with a restaurant kitchen and a live music stage under the same roof.
