Privacy — plain and short

This site has a contact form, an email address, and a map pin. Here is exactly what happens with each.

What this site collects

Only what you choose to send us

When you fill in the contact form, the name, phone, optional email, and message you type are sent to The Donkey Barn so someone can answer your question about tables, groups, or the night's plans. That submission is the only information the site collects from you.

The site sets no cookies, runs no third-party scripts, and does no advertising tracking. There is no account system and nothing to consent to beyond choosing to email or send the form.

The home and Visit pages embed a Google Maps map so you can find 2902 Samson Way; loading that map means your browser talks to Google for the map tiles (see Google's privacy policy). The map does not collect anything on behalf of this site.

We do not sell information gathered here. If you emailed or messaged us and want it removed, write to thedonkeybarn2026@gmail.com and say the word.
