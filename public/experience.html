One barn. Three ways to spend a night.

The Donkey Barn is a country bar, restaurant, and live music destination on Samson Way in Bellevue, Nebraska — built so the kitchen, the stage, and the dance floor all run at once.

The run of show

Dinner first, then whatever the night turns into

Act I
11 AM–late

The grill and the bar

The kitchen opens at 11 with burgers and signature grill plates; the bar runs the whole time with a full beer list and cocktails. This is a working restaurant, not just a drinking hole with a fryer.

Act II
When the stage lights go on

The stage — live bands and DJs

The barn was built as a live music destination. The opening weekend brought live sets from Silas Creek and Whiskey River plus DJ Chris Worms on the decks, across both nights of the Nov 13–14 grand opening.

Live music details →

Act III
After the set

The floor — line dancing under the barn lights

There's a real dance floor, and line dancing is part of what this place is about. Fridays and Saturdays stay open until 2 AM — once the music starts, the floor is the last thing standing.
