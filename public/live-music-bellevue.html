Live music and line dancing in Bellevue, Nebraska

The Donkey Barn was built as a live music destination — a country bar with a stage, a dance floor, and hours that stay open while the music does.

Plan your night

Opening weekend

Silas Creek, Whiskey River, and DJ Chris Worms — Nov 13–14

Two nights — Nov 13 and 14, 4 PM to 2 AM both days: live sets from Silas Creek and Whiskey River, with DJ Chris Worms on the decks in between. That was the bill the grand opening came in on.

The floor

A dance floor made for line dancing

This isn't a bar that happens to have room to sway — line dancing is part of the concept, on a dedicated dance floor, alongside burgers, grill plates, beer, and cocktails. When the set ends, the floor is still the point.

Before you head over

The house rules

Who's on the opening weekend bill?

Live sets from Silas Creek and Whiskey River, with DJ Chris Worms on the decks, across the Nov 13–14 grand opening — 4 PM to 2 AM both nights.

Is there a dance floor?

Yes — The Donkey Barn has a dance floor, and line dancing is part of what the place is about.

How late does it go?

Fridays and Saturdays run 11 AM to 2 AM. Thursdays go to midnight, Wednesdays to 11 PM. Closed Sunday through Tuesday.

Do I have to be 21?

It's 21 and over after 8 PM on Thursdays–Saturdays and during special events.
